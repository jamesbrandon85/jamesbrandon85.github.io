About Me

James Brandon
Photography

I'm James Brandon — a travel and landscape photographer based in Fort Worth, Texas. What started as a passion in 2008 became a decade-long full-time career chasing extraordinary light across the US and beyond.

I led workshops across the country, shot for brands like Sony, Google, Topaz Labs, on1, and Digital Photography School, and built a body of work spanning Hawaiian volcanoes, Great Plains supercells, the Canadian Rockies, coastlines from California to Maine, Europe, and more.

These days I work a full-time job and shoot on the side — but photography never really leaves you. I'm always open to brand collaborations, private workshops, or whatever you have in mind.
